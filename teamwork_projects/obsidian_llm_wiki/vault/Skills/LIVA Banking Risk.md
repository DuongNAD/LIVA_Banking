# LIVA Banking Risk

Canonical instructions live in `.claude/skills/liva-banking-risk/SKILL.md` and the mirrored `.agents/skills/liva-banking-risk/SKILL.md`.

Use this skill to assess corporate financial health and borrower creditworthiness, calculate solvency metrics such as the Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR), evaluate immediate liquidity using the Quick Ratio (Acid-Test Ratio), and model rolling 30/90-day cashflow shortfall scenarios using scaled integer basis point arithmetic.
